description=Click the map (or select points/polygons) and get a chart of NDVI or any index or band through time, from a folder of HLS, Sentinel-2, Landsat or dated GeoTIFF scenes.
about=Cloud-masked time series in a dock: point or polygon, many indices (NDVI, EVI, NDMI, MNDWI, NBR ... or your own formula), several places compared on one chart, a smooth line that breaks at long cloudy gaps, flagged single-date spikes, and a season-by-season view for comparing years. Reads HLS v2.0 (Fmask), Sentinel-2 L2A (SCL), Landsat Collection 2 (QA_PIXEL) and any GeoTIFF with a date in its name. Only the pixels under the point or polygon are read. Needs nothing beyond what QGIS ships. A Processing tool does the same for a whole layer and writes CSVs.
